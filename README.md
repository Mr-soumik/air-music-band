🎵 Air Music Band (Hand Gesture Instrument)An AI-powered computer vision project that turns webcam hand tracking into real-time musical instruments. Play drums, piano, guitar, and bells in thin air using OpenCV, MediaPipe, and Pygame—with zero external audio files required!🌟 Key FeaturesReal-Time Hand Landmark Tracking: Tracks 21 3D hand joints with low latency using Google MediaPipe.4 Virtual Instruments:🥁 Drum Kit: Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Tom, Crash🎹 Piano: C4, D4, E4, F4, G4🎸 Guitar: E2, A2, D3, G3, B3🔔 Bells: Tuned bell harmonicsBuilt-In Sound Synthesis: Generates audio waves on the fly using NumPy formulas (no external .wav or .mp3 files needed).Interactive UI & Mode Switching:Switch instruments by tapping the top screen buttons with your index finger.Or switch instantly using keyboard number keys (1 - 4).Visual Feedback: Real-time indicator showing which instrument and note was triggered.🛠️ Prerequisites & StackPython: 3.9, 3.10, or 3.11Libraries:opencv-python (Webcam capture & UI rendering)mediapipe (Hand landmark detection)pygame (Low-latency audio playback)numpy (Mathematical audio wave synthesis)🚀 Installation & Setup1. 🎮 How to PlayCamera Position: Make sure your webcam is active and your hand is clearly visible.Triggering Notes: Bend or tap any finger downward past its knuckle to trigger its assigned sound.Switching Instruments:Hand Gesture: Hover your index finger over any instrument box (Drum, Piano, Guitar, Bells) at the top of the video feed.Keyboard Keys:1 : Drum Mode2 : Piano Mode3 : Guitar Mode4 : Bells ModeExit: Press Q on your keyboard while the window is focused.🖐️ Finger Note MappingFinger🥁 Drum Kit🎹 Piano🎸 Guitar🔔 BellsThumbKickC4 (261.6 Hz)E2 (82.4 Hz)Bell 1 (523 Hz)IndexSnareD4 (293.7 Hz)A2 (110.0 Hz)Bell 2 (659 Hz)MiddleHi-HatE4 (329.6 Hz)D3 (146.8 Hz)Bell 3 (784 Hz)RingTomF4 (349.2 Hz)G3 (196.0 Hz)Bell 4 (1046 Hz)PinkyCrashG4 (392.0 Hz)B3 (246.9 Hz)Bell 5 (1318 Hz)📁 Project Structureair-music-band/
